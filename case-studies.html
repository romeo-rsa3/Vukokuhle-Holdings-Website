Case Studies

How these solutions play out on real sites.

Every deployment is different, but the shape of the problem — and the platform's answer to it — tends to repeat across sectors.

About these examples: the scenarios below describe how our solutions are typically applied for each sector, based on the deployments we support — they're illustrative, not a specific client's published results. Ask us for reference calls or site visits relevant to your project during your consultation.

Telecom & Network Operators

Multi-site power and fibre monitoring for a regional network operator

The Challenge

A network operator running dozens of tower sites and POPs across multiple regions had no single view of power status, generator fuel levels or cabinet intrusion — faults were often discovered only after a site went down.

Our Approach

Deploy IIoT gateways and environmental sensors at each site, feeding live status into the NEO Central Monitoring Platform with role-based alerting for field, NOC and management teams.

What This Solves

One dashboard for every site's power, environment and access status, with real-time alarms that let field teams respond before a fault becomes an outage.

Municipalities & Utilities

Smart metering rollout with tiered debt recovery

The Challenge

A municipal utility needed to reduce non-revenue water and electricity losses while managing a backlog of historical customer debt, on ageing metering infrastructure that couldn't detect tampering.

Our Approach

Deploy STS-compliant prepaid smart meters with tamper detection, connected through the revenue recovery and vending platform, with tiered vending configured to recover historical debt over time.

What This Solves

Real-time tamper notifications, accurate prepaid billing, and a structured path to recovering historical debt without a single flat rate hike.

Mining & Industrial

AI surveillance and PPE compliance across a industrial site

The Challenge

An industrial site needed continuous visibility over high-risk zones — tracking PPE compliance and unauthorised access — without adding headcount to a control room already stretched thin.

Our Approach

Deploy the Axxon One VMS with PPE compliance detection, risky-posture alerts and perimeter analytics, consolidated into a single command-and-control interface with escalation rules.

What This Solves

Automated compliance alerts reduce reliance on manual review, while consolidated video wall management keeps a small control room team effective across a large site.

Municipalities & Utilities

Consolidating legacy platforms into one municipal system

The Challenge

A municipality was running separate systems for billing, asset management and customer service — none of which talked to each other, creating duplicate data entry and slow query resolution.

Our Approach

Migrate to the ONE unified municipal platform, consolidating asset and vehicle management, smart metering, billing and customer service into a single interface with a companion mobile app.

What This Solves

One system of record for billing and service delivery, reducing duplicate work between departments and giving residents a mobile app for prepaid purchases and queries.
