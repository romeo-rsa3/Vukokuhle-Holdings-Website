Frequently Asked Questions

Answers before you pick up the phone.

If your question isn't covered here, it's a good reason to talk to us directly — get in touch.

We build and operate a unified automation and intelligence platform for telecommunications, municipal and industrial infrastructure. In practice that means remote site monitoring, AI-powered video security, smart metering and revenue recovery, load management, and hybrid IT/OT cybersecurity — all visible from one central dashboard (NEO) instead of separate systems for each vendor.

No. Our platform is vendor-agnostic by design — it integrates with the cameras, meters, sensors and controllers you already have via open protocols (BACnet, Modbus, OPC, SNMP, HTTP/gRPC APIs) rather than forcing a single proprietary ecosystem. Where new hardware genuinely adds value, we'll say so; where it doesn't, we'll connect what's already on site.

NEO is our cloud-based, single-pane dashboard that consolidates live alarms, device status and multi-site views across power, environmental, access control and utility systems. It gives operations, security and management teams role-based access and reporting from one interface instead of stitching together separate vendor portals.

Primarily telecommunications and network operators (tower sites, fibre networks, data centres), municipalities and utilities (smart metering, revenue recovery, water and electricity services), and mining or industrial operators that need AI surveillance, environmental monitoring and SCADA-level cybersecurity. Our technicians provide 24/7 nationwide on-site and remote support, covering diagnostics, repairs, replacements and technical advice to keep systems at continuous operational uptime. Response-time commitments are confirmed per contract based on site criticality and SLA tier — ask us for the specific terms during your consultation.

Cybersecurity is built in rather than bolted on. Because our monitoring and metering systems span IT, OT and SCADA environments, we apply encrypted communications, vulnerability assessments and managed security services across the same infrastructure we're already monitoring — not as an isolated add-on.
