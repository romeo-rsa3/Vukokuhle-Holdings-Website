Privacy Policy

Last updated: 26 August 2026

1. Introduction

Vukokuhle Holdings (Pty) Ltd ("Vukokuhle", "we", "us") respects your privacy and is committed to protecting personal information in line with South Africa's Protection of Personal Information Act (POPIA). This policy explains what we collect through this website, why, and the choices you have.

2. Information We Collect

We collect information you provide directly, such as through the contact form (name, company, email, phone number, message and area of interest). We also collect limited technical information automatically via analytics tools, such as pages visited, general location (city/region level), device type and referring site.

3. How We Use Your Information

  • To respond to enquiries submitted through the contact form.
  • To understand how visitors use this site, so we can improve it.
  • To meet legal, regulatory or contractual obligations.

We do not sell personal information to third parties.

4. Cookies & Analytics

This site uses Google Analytics to understand aggregate visitor behaviour. Google Analytics uses cookies to collect anonymised usage data. You can opt out of Google Analytics tracking using your browser settings or available opt-out browser extensions. This site also embeds Google Maps on the Contact page, which may set its own cookies when loaded — governed by Google's own privacy policy.

5. Sharing of Information

We may share information with service providers who help us operate this website or deliver our services (e.g. hosting, email, analytics providers), under obligations to protect it. We may also disclose information where required by law.

6. Data Retention

We retain enquiry information for as long as needed to respond to your request and maintain business records, after which it is securely deleted or anonymised.

7. Your Rights

Under POPIA, you have the right to access, correct or request deletion of your personal information held by us, and to object to certain processing. To exercise these rights, contact us using the details below.

8. Security

We take reasonable technical and organisational measures to protect personal information against loss, misuse or unauthorised access. No method of transmission over the internet is completely secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

9. Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy from time to time. The "Last updated" date at the top reflects the most recent revision.

10. Contact Us

For any privacy-related questions or requests, contact us at vukile@vukokuhle.co.za or 010 443 0097, or write to us at 5 Thora Crescent, Wynberg, Sandton, Gauteng, 2090, South Africa.

This policy is provided as general guidance and does not constitute legal advice. We recommend having it reviewed by a qualified attorney to confirm full compliance with POPIA for your specific business operations.
